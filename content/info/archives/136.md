以前提到了Google Earth上的一些有趣的地图。那么多数人使用Google Earth最想做什么呢？[googleearthhacks](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/)又做了番总结。注意这里使用的是最新版的Google Earth。让我们来看看是否想的一样。  
10.[找家](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/10.php)，只是中国地图很多地方分辨率不高。  
9.[寻找飞行器](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/9.php)，国内的军事站点也经常做，这里作者提供了[一大堆](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat24/Aircraft-in-flight.htm),人肉引擎果然强!  
8.[在地图上飞行](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/8.php)。Google Earth V4可使用飞行摇杆在地图上转悠，设置方式[Tools] --> [Options]，选择"Navigation"，在"Controller Settings"下选择'Enable Controller'，"Navigation Mode"下选择"Flight Control" ，就可以了。回到正常模式选择"Pan and Zoom" 。  
7.[离奇的地图](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/7.php)。举例：[不可思议的城堡](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile607/Strange-Towers.htm)，[中国沙漠中出现奇怪的蓝色](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile18948/Blue-quirks-in-China.htm)（可能是卫星问题),[UFO](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile2009/6-UFOs.htm),[更多](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat12/Strange-Map-Quirks.htm)。  
6.[奇怪的物体](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/6.php)。[耶稣在秘鲁](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile546/Optical-Illusion-(Jesus)-in-Peru.htm)，[Bob the Builde](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile680/Who-can-find-Bob-down-under.htm)，[远古太空飞船](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile19656/ancient-alien-spacecraft.htm)，[Pacman Invades](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile21163/pacman-invades-.htm)，[其它](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat17/Other-Natural-Formations.htm)。  
5.[具有历史意义的地点](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/5.php)。[日本偷袭夏威夷](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile4024/Pearl-Harbor-X-(Overlay),-December-10,-1941.htm)，[诺曼底登陆](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile7158/D-Day-Invasion-Normandy-WW-II.htm)，[其它](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat40/Sightseeing:-Historical-Placemarks.htm)。  
4.[电视或电影中的场景](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/4.php)。[Tom Hanks的小岛漂流](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile2856/Movie-Cast-Away-Location.htm)，[美剧《迷失》之处](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile16871/lost-oceanic-flight-815-flight-path.htm)，[更多](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat42/TV-show/movie-locations.htm)。  
3.[天气情况](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/3.php)。[红外线卫星地图](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlfile494/Global-Infrared-Satellite-Images.htm)，更多的[实时天气情况](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat45p4/Weather:-Real-time-Conditions.htm)及[天气预报](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/dlcat47/Weather:-Forecasts.htm)。  
2.[著名的标志性地点](http://www.googleearthhacks.com/ten-things-with-Google-Earth/2.php)。
